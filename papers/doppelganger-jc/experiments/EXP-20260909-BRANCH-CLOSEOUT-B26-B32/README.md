## B30–B32: progress and limits

B30 supported its directional target-construction interaction: J=7/32, with
minimum leave-one-context-out J=1/7. Active-target answers were 320/320 correct,
versus 304/320 passive-target answers. The manipulation bundles several linguistic
properties; it does not identify pure voice causality or validate automatic rewriting.

B31 failed its prespecified prediction: K=0. Both component flip rates changed by
5/32, cancelling in the difference-in-differences. Distractor passivization yielded
7 rescues and 19 new harms. Its negative result remains unchanged.

B32 passed its **single conjunctive directional criterion** in AU/NO_DYES:

| Shared entity's position inside R2 | Reversals: CONFLICT | Reversals: ALIGNED | Difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FIRST | 11/32 | 0/32 | 11/32 |
| SECOND | 14/32 | 6/32 | 1/4 |

All 19 primary reversal reductions became correct answers. However, the SECOND
effect came entirely from target-last records. Only two of eight contexts had
strictly positive differences in both mention strata. Leave-one-context-out
minima were 1/4 and 3/14; these are classification bounds, not confidence intervals.

Across all 512 role comparisons B32 produced 38 rescues and two new harms.
Both new harms appeared in UA: one copied a distractor participant into the target
answer; another assigned the same person to both roles. Four of 36 capability
references failed. Total content was 1,036 correct, 48 reversed and four other
wrong answers, despite complete format/key-order compliance.

The separate fixed-input AU-to-UA comparison also happened to produce 38 rescues
and two harms. It is a different contrast and must not be pooled with role-change
results. A decrease in paired flip F can mean both output schemas are now wrong;
F=0 can also hide two simultaneous rescues. Direct reversal R likewise cannot cover
all other error types. Full content and harm accounting remain necessary.

## Evidence grade and next-stage judgment

B30–B32 use the same eight exposed Chinese author contexts, six exposed predicates
and one Qwen3-8B snapshot. Role relation crossed with mention position determines
R2 voice; a voice-by-position interaction remains an alternative explanation.
ALIGNED changes R2 event roles, so it is not a meaning-preserving repair method.
These results offer a more specific within-material failure diagnostic, not a
general mechanism, independent confirmation, or universal intervention.

The sequence yielded useful falsification and sharper counterexamples. The next
priority is consolidation, construct validity and independent-material evaluation,
not indefinite condition expansion on these same items. New experiments stopped
after B32; no B33 was run or authorized in this continuation.
