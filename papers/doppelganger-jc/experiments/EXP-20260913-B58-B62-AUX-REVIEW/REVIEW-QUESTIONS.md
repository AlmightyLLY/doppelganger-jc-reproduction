# Questions for an independent review

Please first inspect concrete input/output records and paired statistics, rather than treating the narrative or earlier Pro/Claude interpretations as ground truth. Distinguish observations, compatible explanations, and claims ruled out by the design. Report which files you could actually inspect. Prior external predictions were useful hypotheses, not independent data validation.

1. **What is the smallest defensible central question now?** Is the strongest route still discourse-syntax sensitivity in role extraction, a task-calibration/accounting study, or a narrower conjunction? Which specific B58–B62 result survives the most plausible alternatives?
2. **How much of the intended linguistic interpretation is licensed?** BA/BEI construction and mention order covary at fixed facts; OSV topic materials and the four demonstration examples have no independent human validation. Specify the smallest naturalness/role-interpretation review and matched contrast that would distinguish linear ordering, lexical/marker recurrence, discourse framing, and construction-level transfer. Do not label the existing tests a causal corpus-frequency control.
3. **How should we interpret B60 versus B62?** The same fixed package hurts Apertus and helps Qwen, with a fresh-material replication for Qwen. Does this make the package a useful diagnostic intervention, a limited method candidate, or mostly a calibration control? What transfer or matched-example ablation would make that distinction without another open-ended prompt search?
4. **What do the AUX failures and negative probe result imply?** NONE held-out decoding is near chance, even though late conditional token readouts often favor author-consistent continuations. What explanations arise from probe parameterization, held-out names, position choice and teacher-forced prefixes? What inexpensive offline check should precede a new GPU experiment? Treat failed decoding as a limit of this decoder, not proof of absent information.
5. **What does successful fixed-shape SELF calibration authorize scientifically?** It passes eight frozen cases, 64 saved-state candidate comparisons and generated-token controls, while retaining five old reversals. Which first donor/sham design could be informative after task/probe validity is established? What would falsify the causal interpretation? Tool readiness is not an instruction to run that design now.
6. **Where is the contribution boundary against existing work?** If you do a literature check, verify primary sources and dates. Generic right-entities/wrong-roles and broad order sensitivity are insufficient novelty claims. Identify the exact controlled distinction that is both unclaimed elsewhere and genuinely evidenced here; say if none is established. Do not treat an anonymous manuscript or an external review as permission to reproduce private material.
7. **How should the next two weeks be allocated?** Rank material validation, one held-out behavioral replication/ablation, decoder diagnosis and mechanism work. Set explicit success/failure criteria and a stop decision. Avoid increasing models or experiment count without a discriminating purpose.
8. **Is a January submission targeting ACL Main scientifically realistic?** Separate current evidence quality from the amount still missing and from official submission eligibility. If discussing deadline dates, verify the current official ARR/ACL schedule rather than inventing a January day. Give a credible minimum strong-analysis paper, the extra evidence for a stronger paper, and reasons to change route or timetable. A general repair method is not assumed necessary.

Please end with: (a) claims we should keep, narrow, or withdraw; (b) the single next decisive test and the predicted outcomes under competing explanations; (c) a realistic high/low scientific outcome. The user will choose the next framework after this review.
