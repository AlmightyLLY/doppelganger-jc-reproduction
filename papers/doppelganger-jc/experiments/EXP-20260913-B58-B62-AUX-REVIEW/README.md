# B58–B62 and role-binding probe: evidence for an external route review

**13 September 2026 · Chinese actor/undergoer extraction · development evidence, not a validated benchmark**

The current question is: **when does the wording/construction of an irrelevant record change role extraction from an unchanged target, and which responses transfer beyond the examples on which they were chosen?** This package follows the [B53–B57 review](../EXP-20260911-B53-B57-REVIEW/README.md). It contains **4,520 formal B58–B62 outputs**, plus a separate Apertus internal-observation experiment and its fixed-shape calibration repair. Raw answers, counterexamples, unresolved cases, actual layer readouts, full probe predictions, and archive commitments are retained.

**What changed:** a broad protective-background/linear-order story became less defensible; a fixed demonstration package showed a reproducible but model-limited benefit. Internal probing has not yet supplied a reliable role decoder or a causal explanation. Calibration of the intervention tool now passes on eight frozen examples, while the original five role reversals in those examples remain.

In B62, the primary paired gain is **20.83 percentage points**, with leave-one-group range **18.18–22.73 points** and frozen group-bootstrap 95% interval **8.33–33.33 points**. NONE original/role-swap joint correctness rises 47→48/48. These are 12 author-group summaries, not 576 independent semantic observations. ## Claims and boundaries

**Supported at development scope:** irrelevant-record phrasing can change extraction from an unchanged target in these synthetic materials; effects depend on target construction, answer schema, model and task setup. A fixed four-example package benefits the tested Qwen snapshot in both orders and once again on new material. Apertus's errors persist under a labelled non-JSON interface, and the same demonstration package can harm it.

**Not established:** a single unified competition mechanism; causal separation of corpus frequency from word order; abstract structural priming rather than other wording/attention effects; a generally reliable repair; an internal correct role representation that is later overridden; a human-validated diagnostic dataset. At fixed Mandarin BA/BEI facts, construction and linear order change together. The OSV materials and examples still need independent naturalness and role-interpretation review.

**Why AUX does not close the mechanism question:** its lens comparisons condition on answer prefixes, its fixed probe has near-chance held-out NONE accuracy, and its background labels are balanced globally but fixed within author group. A late token preference is neither a complete answer distribution nor proof that the model uses a correct representation. The repaired SELF control validates unchanged-state reinjection, not donor-based causal intervention.
